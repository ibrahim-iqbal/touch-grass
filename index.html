how it works

what do i do?

tap the mic and say your time, energy, and where you are — e.g. "20 minutes, high energy, near the park". you'll get one dare to go do.

how does a dare count?

tap "prove it outside", snap a photo once you're actually out there. that's what counts it toward your dares, streak, and stats — just hearing the dare doesn't.

what's the streak?

one proof photo per day keeps it going. miss a day and it resets to zero, so the pips track a run of consecutive days, not total dares.

no mic or camera — now what?

no mic support falls back to a random dare automatically. no camera just means you can't submit proof on that device — everything else still works.

is my data private?

everything lives in your browser's local storage, nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste an api key in settings for nicer voice or AI-generated dares — those go straight from your browser to that provider, never through us.

settings

without a token, dares come from a curated offline list instead of the live model. nothing you type here leaves your browser.

without a key, voice falls back to your browser's built-in speech engine.
